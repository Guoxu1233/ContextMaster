Method

Growing History. Fixed Read Budget.

ContextMaster separates persistent visual history from request-local constraints and distills dense context behavior into a sparse four-step model.
Unified IMVC

Reference, history, source, and target tokens share one role-aware representation.

Fixed-Budget Routing

ConstraintSink preserves mandatory conditions while dynamic routing retrieves relevant history within 6 FE.

PCD → DMD

A dense teacher first transfers full-context behavior; deployment-matched refinement then restores detail on sparse rollouts.

The reference images and videos are sourced from public domains or generated by models and are used solely for research demonstration.
